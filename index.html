01 / The premise

Before the internet,
there was the shop.

'90s Magic is a single-player card-shop adventure set in the loud, strange, wonderful golden age of Magic: The Gathering.

Buy boosters. Chase legends. Meet the local regulars. Build a deck from the sets that started it all, then take it to the tables and see what happens.
